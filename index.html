Seen. Fixed.

Three steps from a hazard to a repair.

01 · Say it

Tap and talk

No forms, no dropdowns, no account required. Describe the problem out loud in any language and add a photo if you want to.

02 · Seen

Mapped, ranked and routed

SayCity transcribes and translates the report, pins its location, merges duplicates, scores the risk and sends it to the right department.

03 · Fixed

The loop closes

Crews see the most dangerous problems first. You get a status update when the work is scheduled and again when it's done.

What people report

The everyday problems that turn into injuries when they wait.

Each report gets a service code so it lands with the team that owns it.

LGT

Streetlights

Dark crosswalks, flickering poles, entire blocks without light after sunset.

PTH

Potholes & road damage

Craters, sinkholes and cracked asphalt that damage cars and throw cyclists.

SWK

Sidewalks & curb ramps

Heaved slabs, missing ramps and blocked paths that shut out wheelchairs, walkers and strollers.

SIG

Traffic signals

Dead signals, stuck walk lights and timing problems at busy intersections.

DRN

Drainage & flooding

Clogged storm drains and pooled water that freezes into ice in winter.

SGN

Signs & debris

Knocked-down stop signs, fallen branches and debris in the travel lane.

Why real time

A report made on the spot carries what a crew needs.

The person standing next to the hazard knows exactly where it is and how bad it is. Today that knowledge gets lost in phone queues, web forms and language barriers. SayCity captures it in the moment and puts it in front of the people who can act.

For residents

Report in seconds, in your own language, and see what happens next. No hold music, no guessing which department to call.

For cities

A live, ranked queue of clean, located, deduplicated reports from every neighborhood, including the ones that rarely call in.
