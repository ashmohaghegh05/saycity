For cities & agencies

One live map of everything your residents are telling you.

SayCity gives public works, transportation and utility teams a ranked, real-time queue of clean reports. The most dangerous problems rise to the top on their own, so crews spend their time fixing instead of sorting.

What changes

Faster fixes, fewer injuries, better decisions.

Shorter wait times

Reports arrive already categorized, located and assigned. The manual sorting step between a call and a crew disappears.

Fewer injuries

Severity scoring pushes hazards near schools, crosswalks and transit to the front, before someone trips, crashes or falls.

Smarter spending

Patterns by block and by season show where repeat repairs point to a bigger capital project.

More trust

Residents see their report move from received to fixed. Neighborhoods that rarely called 311 start to be heard.

Fits what you run

No rip-and-replace.

SayCity connects to the tools your departments already depend on. Tickets flow in as structured records, and status changes flow back out to residents.
